Cashier quick guide

Find or scan a product

Use the large product search on the POS. Press F3 to focus barcode scanning. Ordinary whole-unit product tiles add directly to the basket.

Hold a customer basket

Use Hold Sale to park the current basket and serve the next customer. Resume the same basket later; recorded partial payments remain attached to it.

Take payment

Choose Cash, Card or Mobile Money and record only the amount actually collected. Split and partial payments remain on the same sale.

Returns and cancellation

Supervisor approval is required for refunds. A part-paid basket must be fully reconciled before it can be cancelled.

Keyboard shortcuts

F2
Focus product search
F3
Focus barcode scanning
Esc
Clear the active product search

Register safety

Use Lock terminal when you leave the register. Do not share a cashier PIN or use another worker's session.
