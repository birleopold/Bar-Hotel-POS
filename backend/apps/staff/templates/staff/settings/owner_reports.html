How reports work

Daily reports cover the previous local date. Weekly reports cover the previous seven days. Monthly reports cover the previous calendar month.

The scheduler checks every hour. It keeps one record for each scheduled period and retries recorded failures up to three times. If email acceptance is uncertain, a separate administrator should check the mail system before any manual resend.

The PDF and CSV summarize recorded payments, refunds and merchandise profitability using sale-time cost snapshots. Cost gaps are shown rather than replaced with current cost. The report still does not confirm cash drawer balances, bank settlement, Uganda tax filings, full operating profit, or current stock valuation.
