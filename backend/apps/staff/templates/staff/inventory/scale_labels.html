Before enabling a scale

Verify an actual label from your weighing scale against this exact format. The label encodes net grams; checkout calculates weight in kilograms and uses the configured price per kilogram, plus the existing tax rules. No automatic tare, calibration, hardware control or price-encoded label format is claimed.

Only active, inventory-tracked kilogram products appear here. The prefix and product number are unique within this business. Enter both fields to enable a mapping, or clear both to disable it.
