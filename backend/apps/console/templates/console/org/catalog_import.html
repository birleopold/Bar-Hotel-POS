Move to or from another platform

Inbound adapters currently cover Shopify product CSV, Square item-library CSV, QuickBooks Online products/services CSV and WooCommerce product CSV. Export profiles are provided for the same targets plus our neutral migration format.

Export neutral migration CSV Export for Shopify Export for Square Export for QuickBooks Online Export for WooCommerce

Target platforms change their import schemas. These exports use the adapter version shown in our migration audit and should be previewed in the target platform before final import. Stock quantities are intentionally excluded from product migration files.

Native CSV columns

category, name and unit_price.

Optional columns

sku, barcode, track_inventory, reorder_level, unit_of_measure, tax_rate_percent, description.

Use yes/no for tracking stock. Price supports two decimal places. Quantity reorder levels support three. Unit options: each, kg, lb or liter. New categories are created automatically.

Before going live

Importing products does not create inventory, affect accounting or charge customers. Record opening stock through your existing Stock or Receiving workflow, then review prices, tax settings and staff permissions.

This tool is restricted to workspace owners and administrators. Keep your CSV files free of customer personal information.
