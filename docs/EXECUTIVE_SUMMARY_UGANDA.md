# Executive summary — Uganda-fit hospitality & retail operations platform

## One sentence
A single workspace for Ugandan businesses to run **branches**, **sections** (bar, kitchen, shop floor, front desk), **orders**, **stock**, **rooms**, **services**, and **team access**—with clear handoffs between prep, service, and management, without forcing every site to behave like a generic POS.

## The problem
Many hotels, lodges, mixed bars/restaurants, supermarkets, and service businesses still juggle spreadsheets, separate tills, and informal communication. Money and responsibility blur across **food**, **drinks**, **retail**, **spa/services**, and **lodging**. Owners need **one truthful picture** of operations while staff only see what they need for their role.

## What we built (today)
- **Multi-branch, multi-section** structure: one business account, many locations, many operational areas per location.
- **Staff app** for day work: orders, prep queue (kitchen/bar routing by item), stock, purchasing, lodging, events, offers (discounts), and workspace team tools.
- **Admin console** for setup: branches & sections, item catalog and services (with packages), room inventory for lodging, guided onboarding.
- **Operational guardrails**: e.g. kitchen-prep status can block closing an order when food is not ready; refunds and sensitive actions tied to roles.
- **Reporting direction**: sales can be viewed **by section** (kitchen vs bar vs services) so mixed venues can reconcile reality, not only a single “till total.”

## What we are not claiming in this summary
Payment rails, mobile money deep integration, and EFRIS/e-invoicing are **out of scope here**; they are planned as a later phase once core operations are stable in production.

## Who it is for
- **Owner / tenant admin**: structure, catalog, services, team, and oversight.
- **Managers / supervisors**: approvals, corrections, and day-end discipline.
- **Frontline staff**: fast order entry, prep screens scoped to their section, stock where relevant.

## Why Uganda
Language and workflows favor **plain business words** (branch, section, stock receive, prep queue) over abstract product jargon. The same owner often runs **more than one line of business** under one brand; the product is shaped for that pattern.

## Success in one line
If staff can run a full day **without leaving the app** for routine tasks, and owners can see **branch and section truth** in one place, the platform is doing its job.

## Suggested talk track (30 seconds)
“We give you one workspace for all your branches and sections—bar, kitchen, shop, spa, rooms—so orders, stock, and handoffs stay connected. Your team sees only their queue; you see the whole picture. Payments and tax integrations can plug in when you are ready.”
